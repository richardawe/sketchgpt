Film's "Realistic" people: Microsoft Rocketbox Avatar Library
https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox  (MIT, text below)

Business_Female_01, Female_Adult_01, Female_Adult_05, Female_Adult_10,
Business_Male_04, Male_Adult_01, Male_Adult_08, Police_Male_01
— rebuilt by scripts/film/build-rocketbox.mjs: FBX to glTF, 3ds Max materials
to standard ones (hair opacity baked into its texture's alpha), centimetres to
metres, textures cut to 1024 px WebP, geometry meshopt-compressed. The shapes,
skeletons and pictures are Microsoft's; nothing else was changed.
