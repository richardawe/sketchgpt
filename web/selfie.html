Draw me

Pick a photo of a face. This page draws it as a moving, hand-drawn caricature.

Your photo stays on this device. The page measures the face, draws it, then forgets the photo. It is not allowed to connect anywhere except this site.
    
  
    Glasses, beards and hats are not drawn yet. Face, hair and person models by MediaPipe, run by LiteRT.js (both Apache-2.0), in this tab. Back to sketchgpt
